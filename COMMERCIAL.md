# Commercial licensing

VFX Forge is **public source** and **free for noncommercial use**.

You may read the code, fork the repository, contribute pull requests, learn from it, and use it for personal, hobby, educational, and other noncommercial purposes under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## When you need a commercial license

You need a paid commercial license from Superlapie if you use VFX Forge — including any part of its source, CLI, GUI, schema, runtime, or exported output pipeline — in connection with a **commercial purpose**.

That generally includes:

- A commercial game or interactive product (including free-to-play or early access titles intended to generate revenue)
- A product or service sold to customers or clients
- Internal studio tooling used to ship commercial products
- SaaS, hosted, or managed offerings built on or around VFX Forge
- Client work where the deliverable is a commercial product
- Redistributing VFX Forge, or a modified/forked version of it, as part of a commercial offering

## What stays free without a commercial license

Noncommercial use remains free, including:

- Personal learning and experimentation
- Hobby projects with no commercial intent
- Contributing bug fixes, docs, examples, and features back to this repository
- Forking for study, local experimentation, or noncommercial community work
- Use by qualifying noncommercial organizations under the PolyForm license terms

## What a commercial license grants

A commercial license is a separate written agreement that grants rights not included in the public noncommercial license, such as:

- Using VFX Forge in a shipped commercial game or product
- Using VFX Forge in a studio pipeline for commercial projects
- Redistribution or embedding in a commercial toolchain, subject to agreed terms

Exact scope, pricing, support, and sublicensing terms are negotiated per customer.

## How to get a commercial license

Contact **Superlapie**:

- Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/Superlapie/VFXForge/discussions) with the category **Q&A** or **General**, or
- Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Superlapie/VFXForge/issues) titled `Commercial license inquiry`

Include:

1. Your name or company
2. What you want to build or ship
3. How VFX Forge would be used (editor only, CLI in CI, exported runtime in game, forked codebase, etc.)
4. Expected team size and distribution model

I review commercial inquiries promptly.
